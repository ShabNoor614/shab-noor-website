About The Author

Shab Noor Rajpoot is a writer and storyteller who believes that every heart carries a story waiting to be written.

Her writing explores love, emotions, relationships, dreams and the quiet moments that often remain unspoken. Through her stories, she hopes to create characters and moments that readers can feel, remember and carry with them.

Writing is not just about telling a story for her; it is about giving emotions a voice and turning feelings into words.
